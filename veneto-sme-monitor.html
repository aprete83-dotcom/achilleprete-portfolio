MATLAB · Economic Research · Veneto, Italy

Veneto SME Monitor

Productivity, financial pressure, and the resilience of small manufacturing businesses.

An independent research project combining firsthand business experience with transparent economic data analysis. The goal is to investigate the conditions small firms face—not assume the causes of their successes or closures.

Research in development · Initial project page

Why this project

A question rooted in experience.

Before moving to the United States and working in information systems, I operated a metalworking business in Italy. I saw how managing operating expenses, fiscal obligations, and delayed or unpaid customer invoices could put pressure on a small company.

That experience motivates this study of Veneto's small-business economy. I want to compare personal observations with official data, understand patterns across manufacturing businesses, and explore how financial stress can affect cash flow.

Personal experience informs the research questions; it is not used as statistical evidence. The analysis will distinguish documented observations, model assumptions, and possible explanations.

Research agenda

Four questions to investigate

01 / BUSINESS DEMOGRAPHY

How are manufacturing firms changing?

Study business registrations, closures, and the available measures of active businesses across Veneto's provinces and industries.

02 / PRODUCTIVITY

What affects long-term competitiveness?

Explore regional productivity, investment, and firm-size patterns using OECD, ISTAT, and Eurostat evidence.

03 / FINANCIAL PRESSURE

How do costs shape resilience?

Examine available fiscal and labor-cost measures, clearly separating national indicators from Veneto-specific data.

04 / CUSTOMER PAYMENTS

What happens when invoices are paid late?

Create a MATLAB cash-flow stress test for small workshops, with clearly labeled illustrative assumptions.

Research workflow

Built with MATLAB, one dataset at a time.

  1. Collect: Locate documented, publicly accessible regional economic datasets.
  2. Validate: Identify geography, industry definitions, units, missing data, and time coverage.
  3. Analyze: Calculate trends, compare provinces, and test financial scenarios in MATLAB.
  4. Publish: Add verified charts, source notes, and reproducible scripts to this page.

Current status

What is available today?

The project scope, research questions, and initial sources are established. The Veneto-specific analytical dataset and results are still being developed.

Charts and numerical findings will be added only after the source data have been downloaded and checked. No demonstration figures are presented as official statistics.
